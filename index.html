The Ark Bible Installation

You do not have to install The Ark Bible app to use it, but if you click Install you agree to allow this app to be installed and store local files on your device. If you click Cancel, no files will be stored locally. The Ark Bible app does not collect or store any personal or tracking data whether it is installed or not. If you change your mind later, you can reset the app by clicking the Reset Defaults button at the bottom of the page, or by clearing your browser history. More information about the files stored can be found if you click on the Help link at the bottom of the page. If you allow the files to be stored locally it will reduce data usage for your internet connection.
