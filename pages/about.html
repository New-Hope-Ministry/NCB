About the ARK

If you love something, let it go! If it loves you, it will return! ...unknown speaker

The wise old saying above is not Biblical, but it has been part of my life since I was a child, and hardly a day goes by that I do not think of it. I believe this is precisely what Jesus was trying to tell us when he said, "For whosoever desires to save his own life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for my sake will find it." This profound statement can be found in Matthew 16:25 and similar verses are found in Mark 8:35 and Luke 9:24. Essentially, I feel that Jesus is trying to teach us that, if we prioritize ourselves and focus solely on preserving our own life, our worldly existence, and our own comfort we will ultimately lose what truly matters, which is our spiritual life and eternal salvation. By surrendering our life to God's purpose, following his teachings, and serving others we will find true life, which is a spiritual and eternal existence. I have omitted all personal information about myself in the construction of The Ark Bible because I do not seek worldly rewards. I seek only to serve God, and I spent many years learning and searching for God before the work of building God's new Ark began.

About the Twenty-First Century Version

Why do I do this work?

🪶--- 1 Chronicles 28:9 "And you my son Solomon, know your father God and serve him with a perfect heart and with a willing mind. Because the Lord searches all hearts, and understands all the imaginations and thoughts. If you seek him, you will find him, but if you forsake him he will throw you away forever."
🪶--- Jeremiah 17:10 "I, the Lord, search the heart and examine the soul to reward every man according to his ways and everything he deserves."
🪶--- Psalm 139:23 "O God, search me and know my heart, try me and know my thoughts."
