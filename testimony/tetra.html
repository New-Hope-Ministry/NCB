1. The Tetragrammaton in Judaism
⚖️--- A pentagram is not a Jewish religious symbol in any form. In some Jewish sects, the Tetragrammaton is the four-letter word for the name of God: "יהוה" or "YHWH", it is not an image of a pentagram unless you are a Satan worshipper or you participate in witchcraft or the occult. But the name "YHWH" is not actually written in most versions of the Torah. You can Search the Torah, and you will not find the words Yahewh or YHWH written in the actual text of the search results.

- It is NEVER pronounced aloud
- It has nothing to do with pentagrams
- It has no geometric symbol associated with it
- It is written in some versions of Scriptural texts, but not most

So if you see a pentagram labeled "Tetragrammaton, it is not Jewish.
