⚖️--- Putting the evidence together the archaeological window points to 1300-1100 BCE when Yahweh worship begins to appear among southern nomadic groups (Shasu). From ancient Egyptian texts they were known as "wanderers" or "Bedouins," often described as brigands and associated with the deity Yahweh or YHWH, potentially linking them to early Israelis or Edomites. They lived in clans, moved with herds for grazing, and were noted in Egyptian records from the 18th Dynasty onward. They interacted with Egyptians and possibly served as mercenaries.
